# NIH Biosketch and SciENcv reference

Use this reference for NIH biographical-sketch work. Verify policy pages before submission because NIH requirements can change.

## Current format

For NIH application, JIT, RPPR, and prior-approval submissions with due dates on or after May 8, 2026, use the Biographical Sketch Common Form together with the NIH Biographical Sketch Supplement. NIH requires the forms to be prepared in SciENcv, which produces the digitally certified PDF output. A locally generated DOCX/PDF is a transfer draft, not the final certified attachment.

Official sources:

- https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/forms-directory/biographical-sketch-common-form
- https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/forms-directory/nih-biographical-sketch-supplement
- https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/implementation-of-new-initiatives-and-policies/common-forms-for-biosketch
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sciencv/

## Common Form fields

- Identifying Information: name, Persistent Identifier (normally ORCID), and current position title.
- Organization and Location: primary organization and city/state or province/country.
- Professional Preparation: organization, location, degree/training, start date, completion date, and field of study; list in reverse chronological order.
- Appointments and Positions: start year, end year, title, organization, department when applicable, and location; include current and relevant domestic/foreign positions according to the current instructions.
- Products: citable and accessible publications, presentations, websites, interventions, datasets, software, curricula, or other products. Include authors, title, release/publication date, URL, persistent identifier, and enclosing publication details where applicable.
- Certification: the investigator certifies that the information is current, accurate, and complete and is not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program. The investigator must sign/date in SciENcv; do not sign for them.

## NIH Supplement fields

- Personal Statement: why the person is well suited for the proposed role.
- Contributions to Science: up to five concise contributions, with relevant products referenced by title/author/year. Do not fabricate contributions or products.
- Honors: relevant academic and professional honors; verify dates, exact award names, and granting organizations.

## Safety and quality rules

- Do not include home address, personal phone, personal email, driver-license number, marital status, or hobbies.
- Separate verified facts from user-provided facts and unresolved facts.
- Never infer an ORCID, eRA Commons username, exact dates, licensure, board certification, foreign affiliation, or award date.
- Preserve the original source document and write a new draft output.
- Validate DOCX structure and scan the rendered PDF for blank or anomalous pages before delivery.
- State clearly that final SciENcv certification and submission review remain the investigator/institution's responsibility.
