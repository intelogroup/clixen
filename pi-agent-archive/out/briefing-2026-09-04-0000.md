## Science

**1. Intermediate-mass "not-quite-primordial" black holes may explain JWST early-universe puzzle**
New Scientist reports astronomers have identified a population of black holes intermediate between stellar remnants and galactic centers, which could explain otherwise puzzling JWST observations. The objects are denser than stellar remnants yet far smaller than supermassive centers — potentially bridging a long-sought gap. Source: New Scientist — https://www.newscientist.com/article/2587559-not-quite-primordial-black-holes-may-explain-baffling-jwst-discovery/

**2. Adaptive ML + biophysical virtual screening accelerates hit identification in drug libraries**
Nature Biotechnology describes an adaptive pipeline combining machine learning with biophysical modeling to prioritize ligands from libraries of millions of compounds, cutting hit-ID time versus traditional high-throughput screens with demonstrated enrichment of active compounds. Source: Nature Biotechnology — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03217-x
