## Science

**1. Scientists discover a massive underground 'thermal anomaly' on Mars**
Researchers report evidence of a large thermal anomaly beneath the Martian surface, linked to a hotter southern mantle. The finding could help explain long-puzzling asymmetries in Martian geology, building on seismic observations from NASA's InSight lander, which showed seismic waves dissipating more quickly in some regions.
 Source: Space.com — https://www.space.com/astronomy/mars/scientists-discover-massive-thermal-anomaly-beneath-the-surface-of-mars
