## Science

**1. Hubble images the N44 superbubble carved by stellar winds 



NASA released the new Hubble portrait of LHA 120-N44 in the Large Magellanic Cloud: a cavity spanning roughly 210 by 140 light-years, hollowed out by stellar winds & supernovae from its central star cluster. Gas compressed into the shell is now forming new stars, making N44 a natural laboratory for star birth. Source: NASA Science — https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/nasas-hubble-spies-superbubble-scene/

**2. Fluorescent probes reveal DNA folding at near-molecular resolution 



Researchers at the Center for Genomic Regulation report probes that pinpoint individual dye molecules to within about 3 nm, close to the width of the DNA double helix, in preserved samples,& tracking how chromatin folds & moves in living cells. Tested on wax-preserved bowel tissue from three cancer patients, the approach could make DNA packaging, a diagnostic signal. Source: SciTechDaily — https://scitechdaily.com/scientists-can-now-see-dna-folding-at-near-molecular-resolution/

**3. Bird magnetoreception gets a quantum-optimal-control framing 



OIST researchers relate the ~50-year-old radical-pair hypothesis of bird navigation to quantum optimal control theory: how weak magnetic fields can steer entangled electron-spin pairs,& shift reaction rates,with the same mathematics that choreographs quantum devices. No decisive experiment yet,& a fresh theoretical lens on one of biology's longest quantum puzzles. Source: phys.org — https://phys.org/news/2026-09-quantum-algorithm-birds-migrate.html

**4. Imec + Diraq: eight silicon spin qubits operate coherently on a 300-mm CMOS line 



Imec announces the first coherent operation of eight silicon MOS spin qubits fabricated with an industrial 300-mm CMOS-compatible foundry process: evidence that standard semiconductor manufacturing can scale silicon quantum processors beyond two-qubit demos. It extends the tracker's Diraq thread toward manufacturable, wafer-scale spin qubits. Source: Imec — https://www.imec-int.com/en/press/imec-and-diraq-demonstrate-first-coherent-operation-eight-silicon-mos-spin-qubits-fabricated
