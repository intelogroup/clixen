The IEA reports sodium-ion batteries are seeing accelerated adoption in grid storage and low-cost EVs, with global manufacturing capacity projected to exceed 100 GWh annually by 2027. However, energy density still lags behind lithium-ion, and supply-chain maturation remains a hurdle for widespread deployment.
