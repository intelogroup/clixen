## Science


**1. Quantum free-fall phase measured for the first time - falling atoms confirm Einstein**

An experiment published in Science Advances reports the first direct measurement of
the quantum phase accumulated by a matter wave that is in free fall.



The team, led by Prof. Ron Folman of Ben-Gurion University of the Negev,and including Nobel
laureate Sir Roger Penrose of Oxford, built a chip-scale matter-wave interferometer that
split a cloud of ultracold atoms into two paths - one held stationary,and one genuinely falling,
and measured their interference.



The measured phase matched Einstein's equivalence-principle prediction to within about 2.5%
experimental uncertainty, offering a new, specific probe of the boundary between quantum mechanics
and general relativity - not a unification,but a rare direct test at that frontier.
