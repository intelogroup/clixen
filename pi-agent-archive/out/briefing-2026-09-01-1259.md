## Science

**1. NASA's Swift rescue mission is underway: first robotic attempt to reboost a science telescope**
NASA's 22-year-old Swift gamma-ray observatory has dropped from roughly 600 km to roughly 360 km, hastened by record solar activity; without help,it would burn up by year's end.. In late June, backed by a $30M NASA contract, Katalyst Space Technologies began the first-ever mission to robotically intercept a telescope, aiming to attach the fridge-sized LINK spacecraft to Swift, using robotic arms and ion thrusters to raise its orbit..

Source: Universe Magazine - https://universemagazine.com/en/httpsscience-nasa-govmissionsswiftswift-boost-missionpartners-nasa-ready-for-june-launch-of-swift-boost-mission

The companies say their personalized mRNA vaccine, used alongside immunotherapy, met key trial goals, helping prevent melanoma from returning in high-risk patients,and curbing its spread. Observers caution that full data from the still-ongoing study has not been released.



Source: CBC News - https://www.cbc.ca/news/health/mrna-melanoma-cancer-vaccine-trial-9.7312277
