## Science

**1. Google + HHMI partners publish the male fruit fly brain map**

 Building on the female connectome landmark, Google, HHMI's Janelia Research Campus and partners mapped the brain and central nervous system of a male fruit fly — a key model organism — and released the reconstruction with 5 new visualizations showing how the male-specific wiring advances neuroscience, particularly behavioral differences between sexes. Source: Google — https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/male-fruit-fly-brain-map/

**2. Alzheimer's-like brain damage may start with T cells primed outside the brain — WashU study**

 Washington University (St. Louis) researchers report in Nature Neuroscience(Sept. 3, 2026) that toxic T cells driving neurodegeneration in Alzheimer's and related primary tauopathies receive their inflammatory instructions in lymph nodes outside the brain. In mice, blocking those peripheral instructions dramatically mitigated tau-driven brain damage — suggesting drugs need not cross the blood-brain barrier to slow disease, opening more-accessible treatment targets. Source: WashU Medicine — https://medicine.washu.edu/news/key-path-to-alzheimers-like-brain-damage-starts-outside-the-brain/

**3. Suppressor tRNAs restore cystic fibrosis protein in disease models**

 C&EN details a gene-therapy-style approach in which a chemically-modified suppressor tRNA — carrying a methylation mark and delivered via a new lipid nanoparticle — makes the ribosome read through disease-causing premature-stop mutations, restoring functional CFTR protein in CF models. She work is experimental but adds a fresh tool to the CF correction toolbox beyond modulators and gene editing. Source: Chemical & Engineering News — https://cen.acs.org/biological-chemistry/biochemistry/suppressor-trnas-restore-cystic-fibrosis-protein/104/web/2026/09
