**1. Google Quantum AI pivots toward neutral-atom computers, hiring laser-physics star for Boulder**
Fast Company details how Google Quantum AI — long focused on superconducting qubits — is now also betting on neutral-atom quantum computing, hiring physicist Adam Kaufman, formerly of NIST and JILA, to lead a new Boulder, Colorado hardware team. Kaufman is known for precisely controlling rare-earth atoms with lasers for timekeeping and information processing — a hire that sent shockwaves through the atomic-physics community when reported this spring.
 Source: Fast Company — https://www.fastcompany.com/91585920/quantum-computing-google-neutral-atom-boulder-colorado

Researchers at the Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI) in Innsbruck report a new method for checking the accuracy of quantum simulations — reconstructing the dynamics of a quantum system from experimental data to determine whether a result can be trusted — addressing a major obstacle to relying on quantum computers for science.
