## AI

**1. OpenAI releases GPT-6 Astra; company frames it near AGI thresholds — but independent benchmarks lag Anthropic**
- OpenAI began rollout of GPT-6 Astra (successor to GPT-5.6 Sol) on Sept 4, describing it for computer use, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. Company president Greg Brockman told reporters the release could be “the point at which AGI arrived,” ending a briefing with “Welcome to the AGI era.” On company benchmarks it scored 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4, 57.9% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and 72.6% on OSWorld 2.0. However, independent measurement by Artificial Analysis placed Astra roughly level with OpenAI’s prior generation and behind Anthropic Claude Fable 5.1 and Meta Muse Spark 1.3 on headline intelligence and coding indices. Source: https://theprint.in/tech/gpt-6-astra-is-here-what-openais-new-model-can-do/3033803/
- Cybersecurity note: Astra is the first model to reach OpenAI’s “Critical” Preparedness Framework threshold, able to find and build working zero-day exploits in internal testing (ExploitBench 100% without safeguards). The company says it found and disclosed two previously unknown vulnerabilities, is deploying production classifiers to check reasoning, and notes those checks can interrupt legitimate work. Source: same.
