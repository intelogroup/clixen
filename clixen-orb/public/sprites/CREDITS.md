# Sprite credits

`idle.png`, `move.png`, `click.png` are derived from "Mascot Bunny Character" by Sunardin (Segel),
https://opengameart.org/content/mascot-bunny-character, licence CC0 (public domain, no credit required).
Frames were shrunk, palette-snapped and the shirt recoloured to amber by `tools/make_sprites.py`.
Regenerate: `.venv/bin/python clixen-orb/tools/make_sprites.py <dir containing Bunny1/>`.
